🚀 **AI Job Market Analytics | Data Analyst Portfolio Project**

I’m excited to share my **AI Job Market Analytics** project, where I analyzed job-posting data to understand AI job demand, hiring trends, salaries, job categories, employment types, and geographic distribution.

🛠️ **Tools & What I Used Them For**

📗 **Excel**
• Performed initial data cleaning and validation
• Checked missing values and duplicates
• Standardized fields and verified data quality
• Created calculated fields for analysis
• Performed quick checks and initial analysis

🐍 **Python & Pandas**
• Cleaned and prepared the dataset
• Performed Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
• Validated data types and salary information
• Created fields such as Average Salary, Year, Month, and Country
• Analyzed patterns across AI jobs, salaries, countries and categories

🗄️ **SQL**
• Loaded the cleaned dataset into a SQL database
• Performed business-oriented analysis using filtering, aggregations and subqueries
• Used CTEs and window functions such as **DENSE_RANK()** and **LAG()**
• Analyzed job demand by country, category, company, employment type and year
• Compared AI vs Non-AI jobs and salary patterns

📊 **Power BI**
Built an interactive **3-page dashboard** using DAX measures, KPI cards, charts and slicers.

**Dashboard Pages**
1️⃣ AI Job Market Overview
2️⃣ AI Jobs & Market Trends
3️⃣ Salary Analysis

📈 **Key Insights**
• AI-related jobs represented approximately **60%** of job postings
• The **US and UK** had the highest number of postings
• **IT Jobs** was the largest category with **695 postings**
• **Machine Learning Engineer** was the most common AI-related job title
• Salary patterns varied significantly across countries and currencies
• The dataset contains a large concentration of postings in **2026**, which is important when interpreting yearly trends

🔄 **Project Workflow**

Raw Data → Excel Cleaning & Validation → Python EDA → SQL Business Analysis → Power BI Dashboard → Business Insights

This project helped me understand the complete Data Analyst workflow and, more importantly, how to **validate data, answer business questions, identify patterns, and communicate insights** rather than simply creating charts or writing queries.
